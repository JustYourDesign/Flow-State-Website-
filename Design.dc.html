What we do

BMW servicing and proper repairs, under one roof.

Describe the problem when you book and we'll point you in the right direction.

BMW servicing

Minor and major services with oil, filters, fluids and a thorough multi-point check, so small issues are caught before they grow.

BMW diagnostics

Warning light on the dash? We scan the car's systems and trace the fault properly instead of guessing with parts.

Brakes & suspension

Pads, discs, shocks and bushes inspected and replaced when needed, for a car that stops straight and rides the way it should.

Engine & cooling

From leaks and overheating to rough running and strange noises: honest repairs that deal with the cause, not just the symptom.

Gearbox & clutch

Slipping clutch, rough shifts or vibration under load? We check the drivetrain end to end and advise on the right fix.

Electrical & batteries

Battery and charging-system checks, starting problems and electrical faults, sorted before load shedding nights catch you out.
